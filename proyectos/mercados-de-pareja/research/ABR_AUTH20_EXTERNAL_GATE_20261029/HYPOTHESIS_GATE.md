# ESBM: prospective discriminating hypothesis gate H-RISKSET-VETO (NOT a preregistration or AB cycle)

**Status:** candidate scientifically worthwhile contrast, not established novel. Date 2026-10-29. Zero new analyses, zero outcomes inspected, no predictor fitting on any external data, no seed/search/power experiments. Pending formal scientific originality review and separate preregistration A-J **before** any newly identified confirmatory data outcomes. Do not retrospectively turn this note into an AB cycle.

## What has to be explained

On real encounter e=(i,j,t,s) the observation has to contain a known **eligible offered or held encounter** and native separate second-date yes/no y_ij and y_ji. A mutually positive pair is y_ij*y_ji, and a rejection by one actor vetoes that date despite the other's interest. This is a structural identity, NOT an ESBM discovery. The model must explain the *distribution of that identity across changing risk sets, the order of opportunities and different study populations*.

## Three falsifiable models, to be compared on an INDEPENDENT new original cohort

M0 (mandatory competent comparator): actor-selectivity + target-deseability + session + predatedates risk set + observational event/position effects (cross-classified calibrated hierarchical logit). Conditional q_ij=prob(y_ij=1|X)*prob(y_ji=1|X) as an independence assumption, not guaranteed true. Can include original competent SRM actor/partner effects, regularization and cohort calibration.

M1 (stronger SRM and published literature baseline): add partner-specific relation latent terms, known pair-level similarity and documented predictors such as perceived attraction and interaction cues only if actually observed and temporally admissible; in/out-of-cohort allocation of actor effects defined prospectively. Compare against published Joel/Baxter SRM, Pronk rejection/order, Chang movement/groove, Ogushi RF time windows, Matz language, Zhao permutation and Rios/Saban/Zheng opportunity optimization. No claim that SRM or pairwise dependence is new.

M2 (ESBM mechanistic candidate): **risk-set-conditioned reciprocal veto**, explicitly parameterizing how the distribution of observed opportunities (roster size, attractiveness/demographic features *measured predate*, prior order and within-event congestion) interacts with *two individual choice thresholds* to produce the realized pair-level yes/yes probability. The incremental term must distinguish these mechanisms from M0/M1 with fixed available information and budgets. No retrospective use of partner yes, matches, or postdecision ratings in predating predictions.

Provisional discriminating prediction: when the measured opportunity risk set varies across venues/event cohorts, the joint conditional match calibration of M2 will show an **out-of-cohort improvement beyond M1** after matching date stage and measurement, and an explicit ablation of risk-set variation will remove that improvement. If M2's advantage is absent, vanishes on strong baselines, cannot be separated from actor heterogeneity, or only appears on McMaster 2 sessions, reject/downgrade this hypothesis. This does **not** have verified novelty against optimization and SRM research; novelty audit is pending. In particular, Rios 2022/23 and Pronk 2020 may already cover components.

## Study design requirements before N83

A. Population: real voluntarily attending daters, other venue/sample holdout; contrast actual *meeting* with offer/recommendation/attention, no conflation.
B. Data rights: two independent human samples original files with data-specific permission and SHA256; currently McMaster is source development/exploration **already outcome-exposed** and must not serve as confirmatory holdout.
C. Unit: directed choice nested within real encounter/actor/target/session, only complete original yes/no both sides; missing reciprocal row not coded 0.
D. Timing: predate covariates model ex ante; separate postinteraction models explicitly labelled (postdate Likert only if measured before binary choice). Use no leakage and group-specific missingness.
E. Rival hypotheses and M0/M1 comparators frozen in advance, not weakened retroactively; parsimony and same compute/information budget.
F. Primary test, loss function (proper score e.g. Brier/log loss), confidence intervals/cluster or study-level bootstrap and any multiplicity decision fixed ex ante once event numbers and valid data release known; no arbitrary effect threshold introduced after seeing results.
G. Holdout: entire newly recruited/independent original venue/cohort never explored for outcome values while adapting ESBM; person-disjoint split within McMaster is not independent human collection nor enough.
H. Adverse checks: remove assumed veto dependence / vary predate risk-set construction / calibrated intercept shifts / role thresholds / held-out opponent and event; demonstrate non-imposed result.
I. Measurement/selection: protocol consistency and comparability of yes/no across data sources, consent and eligibility, absent actors, exposure selection, cognitive sequence and interference.
J. If any gate fails, no AB cycle: STOP_R5, retain 24 slots. If strong negative result after genuinely executed A-J, record valid **negative** cycle with unraised CT2; no false 2/3.
