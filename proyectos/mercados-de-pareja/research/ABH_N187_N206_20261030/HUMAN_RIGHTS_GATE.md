# N202 · Original human cohort and rights gate — Hoffmann et al. 2024

**Source newly identified by public publisher page, not a human-data analysis.** DOI `10.1007/s10508-023-02806-0`; original peer-reviewed Springer article published 20 February 2024, title *Sharing and Receiving Eye-Contact Predicts Mate Choice After a 5-Minute Conversation: Evidence from a Speed-Dating Study*.

**Original research design in Methods:** 60 volunteer participants (30 men / 30 women) each attended **four** roughly five-minute encounters against a small set of **four preassigned recurring counterparts**. 240 encounters were collected, 237 retained after exclusion of three for insufficient eye-gaze samples. Participants and their dating partners **each separately indicated yes/no to seeing the counterpart again** after the meeting (native bilateral choices in original study), unlike the sequential proposer/replier logic of ESBM. Two eye-tracking devices captured nonverbal signals; original paper's statistical analyses focused on volunteers' individual intentions, not mutual match outcomes (only ~4% matches) and uses a logistic mixed model. Preassignment and strong reused-counterpart clustering mean 237 encounter records are NOT 237 independent people or cohorts. This is an apparently different study sample from SRC104 Pisanski's vocal dataset; without original recruitment IDs full independence cannot be proved.

**Dataset gate:** paper states data and analysis code are publicly accessible from its OSF project `https://osf.io/vzx6q/`, but original videos were withheld. We deliberately **did not retry any OSF access** (existing project instruction forbids exhausted OSF failures), did not download case-level file bytes, read 0 human rows, accept no license, contact no author. The paper's **CC BY 4.0 text license does not establish a file-level license for original case-level matched votes**, and we have not verified release contents, IDs, native two-column join, complete eligible/exposed opportunity sets, outcome version/date or persistent file checksums.

**Differential ESBM gate:** the genuine human two-vote stage is available in published design description, not original case records. Strong published eye-contact/attractiveness logistic comparator and repeated-counterpart actor effects are mandatory rival features. A proposed ESBM joint outcome `P(send)×P(response|send)` is not automatically the product of two independently cast yes/no after a conversation. Full ESBM vs true SRM/B6 information parity on a new independent holdout is still unavailable. Decision `NO_GO_R5_DATA` / documentary technical N202 only, no 1/3–3/3 scientific grade.

**Verifiable original paper:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10508-023-02806-0 (Methods, Statistical Analyses, Data Availability and Rights); source DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s10508-023-02806-0. The only source inspected was original publisher text; no PDF or original person-level records used.
